Personal care & daily living

Compassionate, personalized assistance.

Olympia Senior Care provides respectful, personalized support with daily activities while promoting comfort, dignity, and independence.

Schedule a consultation Call (360) 968-9752

Every day, around the clock

Our 24/7 team assists with:

  • Medication support according to care plans and regulations
  • Bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, and incontinence care
  • Transfers, mobility, and fall-risk reduction
  • Nutritious meals, snacks, and special diets
  • Laundry, linens, and light housekeeping
  • Companionship, supervision, and reassurance

Care that fits the person

Parkinson's, stroke recovery and hospice.

We also support residents living with Parkinson's disease, recovering from a stroke, or receiving hospice care. Services are tailored to each resident's needs and may include personal care, mobility assistance, comfort measures, and communication with families and healthcare providers.

We encourage residents to take part in daily routines and meaningful activities whenever possible, helping promote confidence and independence.
