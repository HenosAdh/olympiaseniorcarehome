Also part of our care

Every resident, every week.

Mental health care

Our home is licensed by DSHS with the Mental Health specialty designation, and it is managed by a board-certified psychiatric nurse practitioner.

Respite & short stays

A short stay when a family caregiver needs a break, or while your family works out what comes next.

Weekly wellness update

Because you deserve to know how your loved one is truly doing. Each week, our healthcare manager or provider shares an update on their physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual well-being.

Cost and payment

Clear pricing, no surprise fees.

Daily rate

$320 to $470 a day, set after a needs assessment. It covers room and board, utilities, housekeeping, laundry, activities, nursing oversight and all personal care in the care plan.

No extra fees

No move-in fee, no administrative fee, and no move-out cleaning fee. Furniture is provided at no cost if you need it.

Payment

Private pay and long-term care insurance.
