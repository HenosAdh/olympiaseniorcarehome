Recovery care after hospitalization

A safer, more comfortable road to recovery.

Returning home after a hospital or rehabilitation stay can bring relief and new challenges. We provide personalized, attentive support in a comfortable, home-like environment.

Schedule a consultation Call (360) 968-9752

Supporting the transition home

You don't have to manage it alone.

Your loved one may feel weaker, have limited mobility, be adjusting to new medications, or need help with daily activities.

We help residents follow their established care plans, and we provide the assistance, supervision, and encouragement they need during recovery.

What our team can provide

Depending on individual needs and care plans:

  • Assistance with bathing, dressing, and personal care
  • Medication administration according to physician orders and the resident's care plan
  • Assistance with meals and nutrition
  • Support with safe mobility and walking
  • Help with laundry, bed linen changes, and light housekeeping
  • Coordination of transportation and appointments
  • Companionship and emotional support
  • Observation of changes in condition
  • Communication with family members and appropriate healthcare professionals
  • Coordination with physicians, therapists, home health, hospice, and other providers, as appropriate

Encouraging independence

Rebuilding confidence, one routine at a time.

Recovery includes rebuilding confidence and returning to meaningful routines. Based on your loved one's abilities and healthcare plan, we encourage activities such as:

  • Sharing meals
  • Walking
  • Gardening
  • Organizing personal items
  • Enjoying favorite pastimes

Our goal is to support independence while maintaining safety.

Peace of mind for families

When you're working, living out of town, or unable to provide around-the-clock support, our team offers dependable care in a familiar environment. We keep families informed, communicate concerns to appropriate healthcare professionals, and coordinate with the resident's care team when needed.

From hospital to home, with support

We provide a warm, supportive setting where your loved one can recover with dignity, and we help families feel more confident about the care their loved one receives.

Because coming home should feel like coming home, not facing recovery alone.
