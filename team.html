How we hire

Hand-selected memory care experts.

We protect the peace of mind of our families by thoroughly vetting every potential team member before they ever meet a resident.

Stringent multi-stage screening

  • Live Scan background checks and security vetting
  • Intensive in-person interviews and behavioral screening
  • Rigorous skill-based examinations to test care capabilities

Washington licensed and certified

All of our nursing assistants are licensed by the State of Washington and hold active certifications in:

  • Clinical care. Nurse delegation, diabetes management, fundamentals of caregiving, emergency first aid and CPR.
  • Specialized support. Advanced dementia care, mental health support, and care for people living with intellectual disabilities.
  • Daily operations. Certified food handling and safety practices.

Advanced Montessori training

Once selected, our caregivers receive specialized training in Alzheimer's and dementia care. They use compassionate Montessori communication techniques to focus on residents' remaining strengths, understand behavioral changes with clinical empathy, and maintain proactive safety practices throughout our home.

Caregivers here are employees, not contractors. That is what keeps the same familiar faces in front of your parent week after week.
