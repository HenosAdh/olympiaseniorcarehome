End-of-life care

Comfort, dignity, and compassionate support.

When your loved one needs it most, and for the people who love them.

Talk to us Call (360) 968-9752

A deeply personal time

Every person deserves dignity in their final chapter.

The end of life is an emotional and deeply personal time for residents and their families. At Olympia Senior Care, we believe every person deserves to be treated with dignity, compassion, and respect throughout their final chapter.

When a loved one chooses, or needs, to remain in a familiar, home-like environment during the final stage of life, families should not have to navigate that journey alone.

Compassionate care at the end of life

Surrounded by familiar routines and people who know them.

Our team provides a peaceful, supportive environment where your loved one can receive attentive care while remaining surrounded by familiar routines, comforting surroundings, and people who know them.

We work closely with the resident's hospice agency, physicians, nurses, and other healthcare professionals to support the individualized plan of care and help maintain comfort and dignity.

Our approach is person-centered. We focus not only on physical needs, but also on what matters most to the individual: their preferences, routines, relationships, comfort, and personal wishes.

How we support your loved one

Depending on the care plan and needs, our team can assist with:

  • Personal hygiene, bathing, dressing, and toileting
  • Medication support as directed by the care plan and healthcare team
  • Nutrition and hydration support
  • Positioning and mobility assistance
  • Comfortable, familiar daily routines
  • Companionship and emotional support
  • A calm and peaceful home environment
  • Communication and coordination with hospice and healthcare providers
  • Support for family members visiting and spending meaningful time together

Supporting the family

End-of-life care affects the entire family.

Our goal is to give family members reassurance that their loved one is being cared for with patience, compassion, and dignity, while allowing them to focus on being a spouse, daughter, son, grandchild, or friend, not just a caregiver.

We are also here to support families after a resident's passing, including respectful assistance with personal belongings and helping families navigate the transition according to their wishes and applicable requirements.
